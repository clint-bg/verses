41,995 scriptures • t-SNE 2D

This interactive web app lets you explore over 41,995 verses of scripture (Old Testament, New Testament, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price) both chronologically and semantically:

  • 1-Finger Swipe Left / Right (Chronological): Flip sequentially to the next or previous scripture verse (in default Page Flip mode, swiping left goes to the next verse like John 3:16 → John 3:17, and swiping right goes to John 3:15).
  • 2-Finger Swipe Left / Right (t-SNE Similarity): Swipe horizontally with two fingers (or tap the ↑ / ↓ buttons) to step through the 50 scriptures that are closest in meaning based on Euclidean distance in the 2D t-SNE embedding space.

How the Embeddings Were Created

Originally developed using Google's generativeai package, every verse across the standard works (41,995 verses) was processed with text-embedding-004 and embedded into a 2D coordinate space using t-SNE (t-distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding), grouped into 100 clusters.

How to Navigate Verses

👈
1-Finger Swipe Left (or →)

Go to the next chronological scripture in default Page Flip mode (e.g. John 3:16 → John 3:17).

👉
1-Finger Swipe Right (or ←)

Go to the previous chronological scripture in default Page Flip mode (e.g. John 3:16 → John 3:15).

✌️
2-Finger Swipe Left / Right (or ↑ / ↓)

Swipe horizontally with two fingers (or tap the ↑ / ↓ buttons) to step through the 50 closest scriptures by t-SNE distance (#1 closest, #2 closest, … #50).

📜
Long Verses & Bottom Buttons

Scroll long verses vertically with 1 finger, or tap ← Prev Verse (left), ↑ / ↓ Closest (middle), and Next Verse → (right) below the card.
